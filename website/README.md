# Task Force 9

Welcome to the official Task Force 9 website — a home for our milsim community and recruitment information.

## About the community

Task Force 9 is built around disciplined CQB, clear communication, and teamwork. We welcome players who are ready to learn, work with others, and uphold our community standards.

## Get started

Join the [Task Force 9 Discord](https://discord.gg/RkpmHVmQx4) to read the server information and rules, learn about recruitment, and meet the team.

The **Daily Wordle** is a five-letter challenge for Task Force 9 Discord members. Open it as an Activity in Discord or use `/wordle` in the server. Each daily puzzle allows up to six guesses, and your progress is saved to your Discord account.

## Website

This site is hosted with GitHub Pages. The landing page and Daily Wordle screen are designed for desktop and mobile browsers; the game itself is played inside Discord.

For questions about the site or the community, please contact the team in Discord.

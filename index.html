04 QUICK BRIEF

Before you
deploy.

What kind of players are you looking for?

Task Force 9 is aimed at advanced players with at least basic CQB knowledge. We value communication, good judgement, teamwork, and a willingness to improve.

How does the application work?

Join the Discord, read the server information and rules, then use the application channel to start. The bot guides applicants through the form and the recruitment team reviews it.

What happens after I apply?

The recruitment team will review your application. If accepted, you can submit your availability and attend an in-game tryout.

Need a hand before applying?

Join the server and open a support ticket. The team can help answer questions and point you in the right direction.
